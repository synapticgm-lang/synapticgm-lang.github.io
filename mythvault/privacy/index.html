Mythvault: Idle Museum – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026
Applies to: version 1.2.24 and later (Android)

1. Who we are

Mythvault: Idle Museum (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is published by John Pilgrim, trading as SynapticGM.

Contact email: synapticgm@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data ourselves. You do not need an account to play. We never ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • Your museum progress is saved on your device. We never receive it. A grown-up can optionally back it up with Google Play Games (section 5).
  • Purchases (if switched on in your version) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one. By default every ad request is child-directed, suitable for children (rated G) and not personalised. A player aged 13 or over can choose personalised ads in Settings; this only happens after the age question and Google's consent form, and their ads stay rated G.
  • The game does not collect analytics. No analytics service is switched on for any player.
  • We do not sell data, and there is nothing of yours for us to sell.

4. Anonymous analytics (Firebase Analytics)

  • It sends nothing: analytics is not switched on for any player in this version, so no analytics events leave the device.
  • If we ever switch analytics on in a later version, we will update this policy first and tell you in the game.

5. Optional Google Play Games cloud save

The game can back up your museum to Google Play Games. This is optional and never happens by itself. A grown-up must tap the cloud-save button in Settings, after the grown-up check, and sign in with a Google account. Your save file is then stored by Google in that account’s saved games, under Google’s own privacy policy. We cannot see it. If you never tap the button, nothing is uploaded and no Google sign-in is used. You can remove the saved game in the Google Play Games app or in your Google account settings.

6. Feedback form

Settings → Send feedback lets you choose a topic and type a message. A grown-up check comes first. The grown-up then presses send and the message goes over a secure (HTTPS) connection to a Google Form that only we can read. Please do not put names, emails or other personal details in the message. We receive only what is typed, the topic, the app version and build number, and the time Google records. The phone model and Android version are included only if you tick the box. We receive no name, email, account, advertising ID or location. You can send at most one message a minute and five a day. If the phone is offline or the send fails, you can use a pre-filled email in your own email app instead; nothing is sent from it until you press send there, and then we also see your email address as shown by your email app. Google processes the form responses as our service provider, under Google’s own privacy policy.

7. Consent and how to change your choice

Ads are not personalised unless a player aged 13 or over chooses personalised ads in Settings (Settings → Privacy → Personalised ads, which can be turned off again at any time). Analytics are off for every player. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

11. Children

The game is family-friendly and designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, and we do not collect analytics from anyone. Ads are requested as child-directed, rated G and non-personalised for every player under 13, and for everyone who has not chosen personalised ads. Cloud save, feedback, the privacy-policy link and every real-money purchase sit behind a grown-up check. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. We hold no personal data about you apart from any feedback you send us, so most of this you can do yourself: uninstall the game or use Settings → Privacy → Delete my local data; reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings; manage your Google account at myaccount.google.com. For anything else, email synapticgm@gmail.com.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your own country’s data protection authority.

13. Security

Your save is stored in the game’s private app storage. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

14. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
