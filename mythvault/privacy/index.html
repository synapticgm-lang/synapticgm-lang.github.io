Mythvault: Idle Museum – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026
Applies to: version 1.2.5 and later (Android)

1. Who we are

Mythvault: Idle Museum (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is published by John Pilgrim, trading as SynapticGM.

Contact email: synapticgm@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data ourselves. You do not need an account to play. We never ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • Your museum progress is saved on your device. We never receive it. A grown-up can optionally back it up with Google Play Games (section 5).
  • Purchases (if switched on in your version) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one. Ads are requested as suitable for children (rated G) and are not personalised unless you are 16 or over, say so, and agree in Google’s consent form.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics) are collected only if you are 16 or over, have agreed in Google’s consent form, and have switched “Analytics” on in Settings. It is off by default and, for under-16s, it can never be on.
  • We do not sell data, and there is nothing of yours for us to sell.

4. Anonymous analytics (Firebase Analytics)

  • It sends nothing unless all of these are true: analytics is set up in your version of the game, you have said you are 16 or over, you have agreed in Google’s consent form, and you have switched Analytics on in Settings. Under-16s and players who have not answered never send anything.
  • What is sent: a random ID made by the SDK on your device, the app version, device model, operating system version, approximate country (worked out by Google from the IP address), and game events such as tutorial steps, first display of a relic, session start and length, and whether a rewarded ad was shown, completed or failed. There is no free text, name or email in any event.
  • We turn off advertising-ID collection, Android-ID collection and ad-personalisation signals for analytics, and the game does not request the advertising ID permission.
  • Turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Analytics. Nothing further is sent after you switch it off or withdraw consent.
  • Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Optional Google Play Games cloud save

The game can back up your museum to Google Play Games. This is optional and never happens by itself. A grown-up must tap the cloud-save button in Settings, after the grown-up check, and sign in with a Google account. Your save file is then stored by Google in that account’s saved games, under Google’s own privacy policy. We cannot see it. If you never tap the button, nothing is uploaded and no Google sign-in is used. You can remove the saved game in the Google Play Games app or in your Google account settings.

6. Feedback form

Settings → Send feedback lets you choose a topic and type a message. After a grown-up check it opens a pre-filled email in your own email app, addressed to synapticgm@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. We receive only what you choose to type, your email address as shown by your email app, and the app version. The device model and Android version are included only if you tick the box.

7. Consent and how to change your choice

Ad personalisation and analytics are off by default. You can turn on personalised ads only after saying you are 16 or over and completing Google’s consent form. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy. Declining never stops you playing or watching rewarded ads.

11. Children

The game is family-friendly and designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, and we do not collect analytics from anyone under 16. Ads are requested as child-directed, rated G and non-personalised by default. Cloud save, feedback, the privacy-policy link and every real-money purchase sit behind a grown-up check. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. We hold no personal data about you apart from anything you email us, so most of this you can do yourself: uninstall the game or use Settings → Privacy → Delete my local data; reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings; manage your Google account at myaccount.google.com. For anything else, email synapticgm@gmail.com.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your own country’s data protection authority.

13. Security

Your save is stored in the game’s private app storage. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

14. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
